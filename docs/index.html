VOIDLING

The Void is rising. Climb as high as you can. Everyone climbs the same tower, so compare heights with friends.

  1. Jump on aliens to defeat them and bounce higher.
  2. Chomp-dash hits enemies from the side and bites through bullets.
  3. Pick up rocks, bombs and guns. Aim with the mouse and click to throw or shoot.
  4. Find hidden power-ups: shield, super jump, wings, grapple hook and more.
  5. Spiky aliens and red spikes hurt. You have 3 hearts.
  6. Beacons are checkpoints. Collect diamonds: when you die, one brings you back to your last checkpoint (hold up to 3).
  • ← → run
  • Space jump, again to double jump
  • hold Space float
  • Shift throw / shoot (auto-aims, or use the mouse)
  • X chomp-dash
  • G grapple
  • Gamepad: right stick aims
  • P pause
  • ↓ drop down
  • M sound
