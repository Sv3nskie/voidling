How to play

The Void is rising. Climb as high as you can. Everyone climbs the same tower, so compare heights with friends.

  1. Jump on aliens to defeat them and bounce higher.
  2. Chomp-dash hits enemies from the side and bites through bullets.
  3. Collect stones, bombs and guns. You carry them all: Shift uses the selected one, R switches.
  4. Find hidden power-ups: shield, super jump, wings, grapple hook and more.
  5. Spiky aliens and red spikes hurt. You have 3 hearts.
  6. Land on a beacon to make it your checkpoint. Collect diamonds: when you die, one brings you back to your last checkpoint (hold up to 3).
